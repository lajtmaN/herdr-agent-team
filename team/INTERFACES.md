# Interfaces (the contract between agents)

The single source of truth for anything two agents must agree on.
- The **owner of a row edits it** and tells the agents it affects. Lead reviews the diff (PROTOCOL rule 3).
- `TBD(<owner>)` means the owner must fill it in. `EST` means not yet measured or verified.
- Build against what's here, not against what's in someone's head.
